# Assessment model

The proficiency scale is Emerging, Inconsistent, Functional, Strong, and Trusted-Advisor Level. Each capability assessment stores behavioural evidence, communication evidence, strengths, development observations, critical errors, AI suggestion, facilitator final score, rationale, and review provenance.

Feedback has Thinking, Behaviour, and Communication layers and always includes what worked, what reduced effectiveness, behaviour to change, a stronger alternative response, and retry focus.

Judgement & Restraint always requires facilitator approval. Constructive Hypothesis Building also requires sign-off where evaluation depends on business judgement. Overrides retain the AI suggestion and require an override reason.

Use-case discovery does not introduce a new score or change existing weights. A use case may inform a scenario through `useCaseIds[]`, but only immutable scenario-response evidence is evaluated. Facilitator probing turns, assumptions, private gap analysis, and relationship suggestions are never treated as participant performance evidence.
